ABOUT LTBSTC

Education for the whole person.

Love & Truth Bible and Skills Training College is a private Christian institution in Kasosi, beside Love & Truth Church in Bungoma County. Founded in 2026, we unite theological formation with competency-based technical, vocational and professional education.

Our motto is “Empowering Skills, Transforming Lives.” We aim to nurture graduates who are spiritually mature, professionally adept, innovative, entrepreneurial and ready to serve their communities.

ADMISSIONS

Your next step starts here.

We welcome qualified applicants who are ready to learn, respect the College’s Christian identity and contribute positively to the community.

  1. 1

    Check your entry route

    Artisan programmes accept KCPE or KCSE; Certificate programmes require KCSE D (Plain); Diploma programmes require KCSE C- (Minus) or a relevant Certificate.

  2. 2

    Prepare your application

    Complete the official form and provide certified academic documents, ID/Passport/Birth Certificate, passport photos and any programme-specific requirements.

  3. 3

    Register with the College

    Successful applicants receive an admission letter, fee structure, joining instructions, handbook and registration schedule.
