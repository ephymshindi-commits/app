ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Training that meets real life.

We are a TVET-focused college in Bungoma County, Kenya, committed to building useful skills, confidence and good character for work, enterprise and service.

Our learning approach combines guided instruction, workshop practice, assessment and learner support. Course availability, entry requirements and intakes are confirmed directly with the college admissions team.
